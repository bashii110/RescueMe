High severity
Rollover / side roll

Sustained samples≥ 3 within 500 ms
Gyroscope (roll axis)> 4.5 rad/s

Trigger when: peak accel + roll gyro + noise all threshold within 1 s window, followed by device going still.

Noise (dB)65 – 78 dB

Medium severity
Low-speed fall / tip-over
Accel magnitude (peak)14 – 20 m/s²
Gyroscope (roll)2.5 – 4.0 rad/s
Noise (dB)60 – 72 dB
Post-event stillness< 4 m/s² for 4 s
Countdown window45 s (longer recovery)
Stillness confirmation is critical here — bump + roll alone can be sharp corners. Stillness for 4 s confirms actual fall.
Bike note: normal riding produces accel 8–13 m/s² (bumps, potholes). Gyro stays under 1.5 rad/s at speed. Filter out these baselines before applying accident thresholds. Helmeted rider drops noise floor by ~6 dB — add 6 dB correction.
Car accident types & sensor thresholds
High severity
Frontal collision
Accel magnitude (peak)> 20 m/s²
Decel impulse window< 120 ms burst
Gyroscope (pitch)> 1.5 rad/s
Noise (dB)> 85 dB
Post-impact stillness< 3 m/s² for 5 s
Window duration300 ms fusion window
Car cabin dampens sound significantly. Require all 3: decel + gyro + noise. Gyro is lower than bike because car body resists rotation.
High severity
Rollover
Accel magnitude (peak)> 18 m/s²
Gyroscope (roll axis)> 2.8 rad/s
Gyroscope sustained> 1.5 rad/s for 800 ms
Noise (dB)> 80 dB
Gravity vector shift> 60° tilt change
Post-impact stillness< 4 m/s² for 5 s
Gravity vector tilt is the key discriminator for rollover vs side-swipe. Sustained gyro > 800 ms = rolling, not a single jolt.
Medium severity
Rear-end / side-swipe
Accel magnitude (peak)15 – 22 m/s²
Lateral accel (X-axis)> 9 m/s² for side
Gyroscope (yaw)1.0 – 2.5 rad/s
Noise (dB)72 – 85 dB
Impact duration80 – 250 ms
Rear-end produces mostly Z-axis (vertical) jolt in car. Side-swipe produces X-axis lateral + yaw. Differentiate using dominant axis.
Lower severity
Sudden hard stop (no crash)
Accel magnitude (peak)12 – 18 m/s²
Gyroscope (all axes)< 0.8 rad/s
Noise (dB)< 68 dB
Post-event motionResumes > 2 m/s² within 8 s
ActionNo alert — motion resumes
False positive filter: If motion resumes within 8 s after stop, cancel countdown. No gyro + no noise = emergency braking, not crash.
Car note: engine vibration baseline is 5–10 m/s². Speed bumps produce 12–16 m/s² with < 0.5 rad/s gyro. Potholes produce 14–18 m/s² but no sustained gyro. Use the gyro + noise combination to cut these false positives.
Bus / heavy vehicle accident types & sensor thresholds
High severity
Frontal collision
Accel magnitude (peak)> 14 m/s²
Decel impulse> 10 m/s² in < 200 ms
Gyroscope (pitch)> 0.8 rad/s
Noise (dB)> 88 dB
Post-impact stillness< 3 m/s² for 8 s
Heavy mass = lower accel reading for same energy. Lower accel threshold but higher noise threshold — metal crumple on large vehicles is very loud.
High severity
Rollover
Accel magnitude (peak)> 12 m/s²
Gyroscope (roll axis)> 1.2 rad/s
Gyroscope sustained> 0.8 rad/s for 1.5 s
Gravity vector shift> 40° tilt change
Noise (dB)> 85 dB
Post-impact stillness< 3 m/s² for 10 s
Bus rolls slowly due to mass. Lower gyro, much longer sustained window (1.5 s), and stronger tilt check. Longer stillness window = occupants may be slow to respond.
Medium severity
Side collision / T-bone
Accel (lateral X-axis)> 11 m/s²
Gyroscope (yaw)> 0.6 rad/s
Noise (dB)> 82 dB
Impact duration150 – 400 ms
Post-impact stillness< 3 m/s² for 6 s
Side collision on a bus = extreme structural impact. Noise is most reliable indicator here due to the metal-on-metal contact area.
False positive cases
Bus-specific false positives
Passengers boardingAccel spike 6–10 m/s²
Speed bumps (heavy veh.)Accel 10–13 m/s²

Bus false positives need higher accel baseline (12 m/s² floor) and mandatory noise + gyro co-trigger to avoid alerts on stops and bumps.
Bus / truck note: engine idle creates 4–8 m/s² continuous vibration. Raise your smoothing window to 7–10 samples. Also raise average floor to 12 m/s² before processing begins to suppress idle noise.
Universal detection logic — time-based fusion
Phase 1
Pre-event baseline (always running)
Smoothing window5 samples (bike), 7 (car), 10 (bus)
Average accel floor10 m/s² (bike), 11 (car), 12 (bus)
Gyro quiet threshold< 0.3 rad/s = normal
Sample rateSENSOR_DELAY_NORMAL ~50 Hz
Rolling average dropIf avg drops below floor, reset
Only start counting high-accel samples when rolling average clears the floor. Prevents engine vibration from pre-loading the counter.
Phase 2
Impact detection window (300–500 ms)
Accel high-count trigger≥ 2–3 samples above threshold
Gyro confirmationMust exceed per-vehicle min
Noise confirmationMust exceed per-vehicle dB min
All-sensor window300 ms (bike), 400 ms (car), 500 ms (bus)
Axis weightZ dominant = frontal, X = side, Y = up/down
All three sensors must fire within the window. Any two-of-three = medium alert with extended countdown. One-of-three = ignore.
Phase 3
Post-impact confirmation (1–10 s)
Stillness checkAvg accel < 5 m/s² sustained
Stillness window2 s (bike), 5 s (car), 8 s (bus)
Motion resume cancelAvg > 8 m/s² resumes within 8 s → cancel
Tilt confirmationGravity vector shift > 40° → rollover
Action on confirmStart countdown (30 s)
Stillness is the most reliable post-crash indicator. Speed bumps, potholes, even drops all end with immediate resumption of normal motion. A real crash does not.
Phase 4
Countdown + cancellation
Countdown duration30 s (bike/car), 45 s (bus)
Cancel: user input"I'm safe" within countdown
Cancel: motion resumesAuto-cancel if normal motion detected
Cancel: low severityOnly 2/3 sensors → 60 s countdown
Trigger: countdown expiresSend SMS + emergency call
After countdown expires: send SMS, start emergency calls. Never send SMS during phase 2 or 3 alone — always wait for stillness confirmation.
